Steam Scout Alt

Steam reviews broken down by language. A static remake of Steam Scout, rebuilt after Steam retired the old reviews endpoint.

    Counts come from Steam's IUserReviewsService API and are fetched by your own browser, so nothing goes through a server. Steam caches each answer for up to 10 minutes.

    Off-topic review activity (review bombs) is left out unless you tick the box, same as the store page. "Untagged" is whatever Steam counts in the total but doesn't tag with one of its 30 store languages.
